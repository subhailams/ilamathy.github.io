## Problem
Style classification models struggle to capture nuanced visual-textual context in artistic works. Traditional image-only approaches miss rich semantic information from artwork descriptions.

## Solution
Achieved 93% accuracy by integrating image and text representation learning. The multimodal approach captures both visual style features and semantic descriptions.

## Key Features
- **Vision Models**: ResNet, EfficientNet for image features
- **Language Models**: BERT for text embeddings
- **Fusion Strategies**: Early, late, and hybrid fusion
- **Art Domains**: Paintings, sculptures, digital art

## Technical Implementation
- **Tech Stack**: PyTorch, Transformers, Computer Vision, NLP
- **Models**: ResNet50 + BERT with attention fusion
- **Dataset**: WikiArt and custom art collections
- **Training**: Multi-task learning with style and genre classification

## Results
- **93% accuracy** on artistic style classification
- Outperformed image-only and text-only baselines
- Effective style transfer guidance
