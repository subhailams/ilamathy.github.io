## Overview
A comparative study on optimizing DeepSeek R1 through quantization and efficient inference acceleration. This research explores various optimization techniques to make large language models more accessible and efficient.

## Research Focus
- **Quantization Techniques**: INT8, INT4, and mixed-precision quantization
- **Inference Optimization**: TensorRT, vLLM, and LLMCompressor
- **Performance Analysis**: Latency, throughput, and accuracy trade-offs
- **Deployment Strategies**: Edge and cloud deployment considerations

## Technical Implementation
- **Tech Stack**: LLM, LLMCompressor, TensorRT, vLLM, Transformers
- **Benchmarking**: Comprehensive performance evaluation
- **Optimization Pipeline**: End-to-end optimization workflow
- **Deployment**: Production-ready inference systems

## Key Findings
- Quantization impact on model accuracy and speed
- Optimal configurations for different deployment scenarios
- Trade-offs between model size, speed, and quality
