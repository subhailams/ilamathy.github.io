Hi there, I'm **Subha Ilamathy** 👋

I'm an AI Engineer specializing in **Large Language Models**, **Multimodal AI**, **Computer Vision**, and **Recommendation Systems**. With a Master's in Computer Science from the University of Illinois Chicago (4.0 GPA) and hands-on experience at organizations like Zoho Corporation and Multicoreware, I build production-ready AI systems that solve real-world problems.

## What I Do

I develop **agentic AI systems** and **RAG-based solutions** that go beyond traditional chatbots. My work spans:
- 🤖 **Agentic AI**: Building intelligent support systems using CrewAI and multimodal RAG
- 🧠 **GenAI Interfaces**: Creating intuitive multimodal interfaces with speech, gesture, and text
- 📊 **Graph-based AI**: Designing context-aware retrieval systems with Neo4j and knowledge graphs
- 🎯 **ML at Scale**: Optimizing inference for production with TensorRT, quantization, and edge deployment

## Recent Projects

I recently developed **Arta Support**, an AI-driven support co-pilot that automates ticket resolution using multimodal RAG, and **ArGraph**, a graph-based research assistant for Argonne National Laboratory. My work on **VisualGPT** with Adobe Research explores how LLMs can revolutionize creative workflows through natural language image editing.

## Background

Before grad school, I spent nearly 3 years as a **Member Technical Staff** at Zoho Corporation and a **Software Engineer** at Multicoreware, where I built computer vision systems, optimized deep learning models for edge devices, and won the **Smart India Hackathon 2020** with an AI-powered surveillance solution.

I'm passionate about making AI more accessible, efficient, and impactful. Whether it's optimizing DeepSeek R1 for faster inference or building multimodal recommendation systems, I thrive on challenges that push the boundaries of what AI can do.
